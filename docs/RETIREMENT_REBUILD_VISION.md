# Retirement Rebuild — Vision and Governing Principles

**Established:** October 8, 2026  
**Purpose:** Permanent strategic North Star across development work and ChatGPT sessions.  
**Status:** Governing direction, updated November 3, 2026. The charter describes intent, not proof that a capability is implemented.

## Mission

Build an evidence-driven, auditable investment system that **actively hunts for asymmetric, attractive investment opportunities** to pursue substantial risk-aware retirement capital growth. Seek credible upside across appropriate markets and instruments, quantify the price paid against possible outcomes, challenge each thesis independently, choose an appropriate vehicle only after its economics and risks are understood, apply deterministic risk controls, accurately account for authorized transactions, and learn from measured outcomes. Volatility, uncertainty, and possible loss are accepted when adequately compensated; avoiding risk or producing research alone is not success.

**We optimize for investment judgment and long-term reliability, not trade volume, impressive predictions, or unnecessary complexity.** Investing involves loss risk; no process guarantees returns.

## Portfolios and responsibilities

**Real Portfolio — long-term ownership.** AI research and an independent Investment Committee determine ownership merit. The deterministic Retirement Rebuild engine alone sizes BUYs. The user manually executes brokerage trades; actual fills must be recorded and linked to decisions. Protect Real Portfolio data and behavior from AG experiments.

**Accelerated Growth (AG) — aggressive, evidence-based opportunity hunting; currently paper-only.** Actively search for significant capital appreciation from positive business change, valuation dislocation, structural mispricing, and special situations. Aggression means pursuing potentially high returns and accepting meaningful downside when justified by credible upside-versus-downside economics; it does **not** mean manufacturing BUYs, ignoring evidence, or maximizing leverage. Stocks remain central but are not the exclusive opportunity vehicle. Research may compare individual equities, sector/commodity exposure through suitable vehicles, event-driven situations, and **defined-risk listed options** (including long-dated calls and debit spreads) when thesis timing, premium, implied volatility, liquidity, contract size, and maximum loss make them appropriate. Options are a prospective **paper-research track only**; this charter does not authorize real options trading, margin borrowing, naked option selling, or new execution capability. AG retains five independent opportunity *thesis* paths, regardless of instrument:

1. **Accelerating fundamentals:** strengthening revenue, earnings, margins, free cash flow, or unit economics.
2. **Emerging opportunity:** verifiable technology, adoption, business-model, or structural industry developments that could create business value.
3. **Turnaround:** credible operational or financial recovery, even where recent revenue growth is weak or negative.
4. **Catalyst-driven:** a specific development or event with material economic implications and an observable evidence horizon.
5. **Valuation dislocation:** a defensible gap between price and business value, plus a plausible value-realization mechanism; not every thesis needs a discrete scheduled catalyst.

A company can qualify through **any one** path. Each requires credible evidence, a causal economic mechanism, counter-evidence, and observable invalidation conditions.

**Paper Long-Term, Paper Active, and VOO** remain comparison/control portfolios. They must not be altered merely to flatter results.

The working long-run discussion is roughly **75% Real / 25% AG** combined capital. This is strategic context, **not** authorization to transfer funds, rebalance, override existing caps, or trade.

## Nonnegotiable principles

1. **Evidence over narrative.** Distinguish verified facts, inference, and unknowns. Prefer primary filings, financial reports, and verifiable company developments.
2. **Search is accountable; decisions have no quotas.** Do not force a BUY, invest merely to stay active, or reserve slots for an opportunity path. But persistent inability to identify a justified BUY across a sufficiently broad and varied opportunity universe is a **strategy-discovery or decision-calibration failure requiring investigation**, not an acceptable indefinite resting state. Track search coverage, opportunity economics, and reasons for non-selection.
3. **Independent challenge.** Discovery proposes hypotheses; adversarial Deep Research challenges them; the Committee independently decides BUY/WATCH/REJECT. No stage rubber-stamps the previous one.
4. **AI never sizes BUYs.** The Committee judges ownership merit, not dollars, shares, or portfolio weights. Deterministic controls govern sizing, available cash, eligibility, and concentration.
5. **Aggressive but compensated risk-taking.** Assess liquidity, leverage, dilution, valuation, concentration, catalyst timing, expiration, and maximum possible loss as appropriate. Do not equate volatility, uncertainty, or unconventional instruments with uninvestability; distinguish risks that price or deterministic exposure limits can compensate from risks that defeat the thesis.
6. **Fail closed on consequential uncertainty.** Incomplete cycles, missing critical evidence, invalid checkpoints, or conflicting records must not authorize trades.
7. **Actual fills are the accounting truth.** Research decisions are not holdings. Cash, transactions, cost basis, positions, and decision links must reconcile.
8. **Keep an audit trail.** Preserve historical research, sources, versions, decisions, and transactions; supersede or resolve obsolete records rather than silently deleting them.
9. **Protect the Real Portfolio.** Experimental AG research changes must not unexpectedly modify Real execution or accounting.
10. **Scale only after evidence of reliability.** Test progressively; don't interpret a short winning streak as validation.
11. **Favor simple, testable improvements.** Reuse proven components without inheriting inappropriate assumptions.
12. **No unapproved operational changes.** Design conversations and read-only audits are not permission to modify production behavior, deploy, reset records, fund accounts, or execute trades.

## Target decision lifecycle

1. **Opportunity Hunter:** Search a broad, economically meaningful universe for mispricing, inflections, restructurings, special situations, and catalysts across the five thesis paths. Separate true eligibility and liquidity safeguards from candidate attractiveness. Do not select market candidates merely by alphabetical position, price, or volume.
2. **Cheap economics screen:** Evaluate credible potential reward versus plausible downside, current price-implied expectations, an observable value-realization mechanism, and thesis timing before spending heavily on research. Rank research attention by economic opportunity as well as evidence quality; no arbitrary BUY or path quotas.
3. **Independent Deep Research:** Verify issuer and instrument identity, source data, capital structure, earnings/cash conversion, material risks and counter-evidence, thesis invalidation, and whether the upside mechanism is economically plausible.
4. **Decision-grade valuation:** Where supported, construct explicit adverse, base, and favorable scenarios, normalized cash/earnings assumptions, a defensible valuation or return range, and materiality-tested data reconciliation. Disclose uncertainties rather than fabricate precision. Distinguish inconsequential vendor differences from contradictions that could reverse a decision.
5. **Independent Investment Committee:** Decide BUY/WATCH/REJECT based on **ownership economics at the available price**, including opportunity cost of waiting. Do not require favorable thesis confirmation when risks are already adequately priced. WATCH needs a concrete economic reason to wait, not generic uncertainty; REJECT should distinguish failed economics from simply missing a catalyst. A BUY is a research opinion, never a trade order.
6. **Vehicle comparison (research):** When useful, compare outright shares with defined-risk options or other suitable exposure on *risk-adjusted, time-sensitive economics*, including premium, breakeven, volatility, liquidity, spreads, expiration, and full-premium-loss risk. Do not infer an option BUY from a share BUY. Keep alternative-instrument research paper-only pending separate authorization and purpose-built controls.
7. **Deterministic risk and execution boundary:** Apply appropriate size, cash, concentration, loss budget, instrument eligibility, and authorized-workflow controls. AI never sizes or executes a BUY. Real Portfolio execution remains separately governed and manual at the brokerage.
8. **Monitoring and learning:** Preserve source-date snapshots, decision versions, thesis clocks, post-decision outcomes, paper comparison portfolios, and counterfactual results against benchmarks and other candidates. Revisit both bad trades and missed opportunities.

## AG implementation direction and current gap (November 3, 2026)

The five-path Discovery v2, independent Deep Research, saved Committee workflow, and one-ticker targeted tests exist on an **unmerged draft feature branch** as of this update; check GitHub and deployments for live state. This is not proof of production readiness or profitability. The broad AG opportunity pool still samples an eligible alphabetized universe rather than ranking prospects by attractive economics. In the saved results reviewed on November 3, **17 AG Committee decisions were all WATCH** (15 historical v1 and 2 targeted v3), which is an urgent diagnostic finding, not proof that any particular WATCH should have been BUY. Research can identify important risks without quantifying whether the current price compensates for them; vendor reconciliation issues may also obstruct defensible decisions.

**Next initiative: AG Opportunity Hunter and valuation validation.** Before further one-ticker prompt fixes or repeated WATCH retests, audit the selection-to-decision pipeline, the model's price sensitivity, data materiality, and realistic option-vehicle feasibility. Design the smallest generalizable improvement; test on a fixed, date-correct sample of favorable, neutral, and unattractive cases, including buy-now versus wait and price-sensitivity cases. Use actual point-in-time evidence without hindsight. Then run a meaningful breadth-first paper market scan using economically justified screening criteria, and assess whether suitable, well-priced opportunities are surfaced. Do not impose a predetermined number of BUYs; do treat prolonged failure to locate any actionable ideas as a measurable failure to correct.

Preserve source and decision histories; do not silently overwrite old WATCHs or revise in-flight versions. Never allow a paper AG experiment to change Real Portfolio trade behavior. **AG execution, options execution, funding, and broker connectivity remain disabled unless separately and explicitly authorized.**

## Definition of success

Success requires demonstrable ability to **find and discriminate actionable asymmetric opportunities** across a sufficiently broad search, not merely process candidates or document risks. Measure candidate coverage and economic rank quality; source-consistent valuation ranges and downside cases; price-sensitive BUY/WATCH/REJECT calibration; missed-upside opportunity costs; safe instrument selection; post-decision outcomes and performance relative to controls; and auditability, accounting, and execution integrity. A single BUY is not validation, but an ongoing inability to find any justified BUY despite adequate breadth is an explicit failure signal demanding correction. Do not manufacture trades to meet a metric.

Real-money testing began with **$200**. Proposed evidence-based scaling checkpoints include **$500, $1,000, and $2,500** before larger amounts; these are not automatic funding instructions. AG remains paper-only during development.

## How to use this document across sessions

**Read this charter first** when starting or continuing Retirement Rebuild work. Then inspect current repository code, relevant architecture notes, open PRs, and verified operational state. Treat this charter as strategic direction, **not** as a live status report.

Clearly distinguish **agreed principles**, **proposed designs**, **implemented code**, and **observed results**. Keep routine progress and engineering decisions in separate notes or issues.

Before significant changes, ask: Does this advance the mission? Which failure mode does it solve? How will we test it? Does it preserve Real Portfolio isolation, deterministic controls, accounting, and auditability? Is explicit user approval required?

**Change control:** Update this vision only when the user agrees to a meaningful strategic change; date the change and explain why. Do not let day-to-day implementation details displace the mission.
